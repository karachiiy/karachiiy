Illustrator's Note: Perspective & Architecture

Working from Ms. Tsuu's initial composition, the primary tribulation was resolving structural perspective. We adjusted the vertical shelf to sit properly in front of the table to avoid a jarring perspective break, and pulled the table away from the brick oven to prevent a visual fire hazard.

Additionally, we painted over the window beside the oven with solid brick to enforce architectural logic, and refined the flour sack based on the author's precise vision.

Illustrator's Note: Character & Environment Polish

The polish phase involved meticulous cleanup: aligning the carpet, adding depth to the 2D pots, fixing mismatched shelf knobs, and correcting chair lighting.

The largest revision was executing a "South Korean Hot Prince" aesthetic for Alistair. We widened his shoulders and filled out his chest to fit a warrior's build, making his face more masculine to ensure his physical presence matched the author's vision, while maintaining Melina's flawless depiction.
