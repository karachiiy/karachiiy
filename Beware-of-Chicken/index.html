Scroll II — Webtoon Establishing Scene

Arrival at Azure Hills

The very moment Jin Rou arrives at the Azure Hills, a pivotal scene from the official webtoon serialization. It sets the stage for a grand departure from the heavenly realms into the humble, earthly Dao.

Illustrator's Note
To match the rigorous Heavenly Dao of serialization, this paint-over was executed in a staggering 3-day sprint. The composition captures the vibrant vastness of the frontier, contrasting the peaceful hearth of the countryside with the daunting peaks beyond.

ILLUSTRATOR'S NOTE: THE PRINCIPLE OF MA (間)

Notice how the composition inherently guides the eye leftward. By strategically positioning the interface over the rightward forest, we embrace the Eastern concept of Negative Space (Ma). It respectfully anchors the layout, amplifying the focal subjects as they gaze upon their new land without obscuring the Dao of the scene.
