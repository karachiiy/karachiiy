✥ ✦ ✥

LAYLA: "Jade... I specifically asked you to scout the perimeter for five minutes. Five minutes! How did you manage to aggravate an ancient Roc in under three?!"

LAYLA: "THE SIGN WAS THE MOTHER CURRENTLY TRYING TO SWALLOW US WHOLE!"
